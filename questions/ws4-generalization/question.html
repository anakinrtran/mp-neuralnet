A model is only useful on characters it has never seen. In this workspace your model trains on a small training set, 100 characters per class, and a big model will memorize it. You'll watch it overfit, try dropout, weight decay, data augmentation and early stopping, and look past accuracy with a confusion matrix and the model's worst mistakes.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace. Forgot what a block does? Hold your mouse over it for a short reminder.
  3. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade. It grades your latest finished run.

Grading uses a held-out test image: the practice picture written again with handwritten characters that are nowhere in your workspace, so you can't tune your model on them. To pass, your model must color enough of its pixels correctly, trained on at most 100 characters per class. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you. Your feedback will show the decoded held-out image next to the original.
