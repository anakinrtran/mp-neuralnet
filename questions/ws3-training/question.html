A good network can still learn badly. In this workspace the model is already a working CNN, but its training is broken. You'll diagnose it from the loss curve and the Gradients tab, fix it, then compare optimizers, learning rates, batch sizes, loss functions and BatchNorm.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace. Forgot what a block does? Hold your mouse over it for a short reminder.
  3. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade. It grades your latest finished run.

To pass, your model must color enough of the practice image's pixels correctly, trained for at most 2 epochs. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you. Your feedback will show your decoded picture next to the original.
