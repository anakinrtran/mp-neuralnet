Build your first neural network with blocks. It reads handwritten characters (the digits 0–9 and the letters A–Z) and uses them to decode a hidden practice picture.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace: run the starting model, look at its weights, add a hidden layer, and decode the practice image.
  3. In the workspace, click Submit. It saves your latest finished model.
  4. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade.

To pass, your model must color enough of the practice image's pixels correctly. The starting linear model falls short; a model with a hidden layer gets there. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you. Your feedback will show your decoded picture next to the original.
