Workspace 1's network looks at every pixel on its own. A convolutional network looks for small patterns, like edges and curves, wherever they appear in the image. In this workspace you build a convolutional network yourself, watch what its filters learn, and look inside a single training step.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace. Forgot what a block does? Hold your mouse over it for a short reminder.
  3. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade. It grades your latest finished run.

To pass, your model must use at least one Conv layer and color at least 90% of the practice image's pixels correctly. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you, and the workspace's validation accuracy counts them the same way. Your feedback will show your decoded picture next to the original.
