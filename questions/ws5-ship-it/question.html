A model is only useful on characters it has never seen. In this workspace your model trains on a small training set, 100 characters per class, and a big model will memorize it. You'll watch it overfit, try dropout, weight decay, data augmentation and early stopping, and look past accuracy with a confusion matrix and the model's worst mistakes. Then you ship it: try it on a picture of your own, read its model card, and decode the hidden class image.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace. Forgot what a block does? Hold your mouse over it for a short reminder.
  3. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade. It decodes the hidden class image with your latest finished run.

Your feedback shows only your accuracy on the hidden image, never the image itself. To pass, your model must color at least 87% of its pixels correctly, trained on at most 100 characters per class. Your predictions are also saved for the class vote: every pixel takes the color most of the class agrees on, and the combined image is revealed at the end of the course. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you, and the workspace's validation accuracy counts them the same way.
