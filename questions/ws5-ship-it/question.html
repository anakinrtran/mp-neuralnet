Time to ship. Build the best network you can from everything in Workspaces 1–4 and train it on Workspace 4's small training set. Then try it on a picture of your own, read its model card, and decode the hidden class image.

  1. Click Open workspace below. The workspace opens in a new tab.
  2. Follow the Steps tab in the workspace.
  3. Come back to this page and click Save & Grade. It decodes the hidden class image with your latest finished run.

Your feedback shows only your accuracy on the hidden image, never the image itself. To pass, your model must color at least 88% of its pixels correctly. Your predictions are also saved for the class vote: every pixel takes the color most of the class agrees on, and the combined image is revealed at the end of the course. Mixing up look-alike characters (0/O, 1/I, 2/Z, 5/S, 6/G, 8/B) doesn't count against you, and the workspace's validation accuracy counts them the same way.
